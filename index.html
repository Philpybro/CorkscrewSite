About the Game

CORKSCREW sets you in Rural England in the year 2019, where you play as a young 10 year old lad who's work is rudely interrupted by a weird kid from his school (he's the guy in the game with a cat-face stuck to his face like a mask, dodgy guy lmao), who has used the power of evil (and having a fetish) to take over his small nation! And getting under the young lad's skin by taking the one thing he loves the most, his Cash! The Player must traverse the levels to not only grab back his cash, but to give this weird kid a right walloping!

The game draws inspiration from the creator's childhood comics, featuring wacky adventures that have evolved into the modern canon during game development. Check out the comic series on GlobalComix for more backstory.

Developed by CharlieDoesStuff, coming soon to Early Access on Steam. For more details, check out the comic series on GlobalComix.
